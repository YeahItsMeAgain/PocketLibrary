    06 · What is real here

    The arithmetic and the measurements are real. The back cover is generated.

    Spine arithmetic

    spine = pages / 2 × caliper + 1.2 mm. The 1.2 mm is the cover allowance for two cover panels. A 320-page novel on 0.100 mm cream stock therefore gets 17.2 mm, not a hand-entered inch.

    Wrap arithmetic

    wrap width = 2 × trim + spine + 2 × bleed and height = trim + 2 × bleed. Panels meet at the fold; the spine is painted last with a 1 px overlap so there is no seam.

    Type fit

    Canvas measureText supplies the real glyph box and advance width. The fit takes the smaller of the across-spine and along-spine limits, then reports the ratio. If it cannot reach 7 pt it says so and falls back.
