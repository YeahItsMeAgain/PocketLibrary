Popular spines the app can ship.

A curated pack of textless art and spine crops, matched by ISBN, content hash or title+author, verified with SHA-256, cached in IndexedDB and rendered by the same v6/v7 measured type engine. When an edition is missing, corrupt, rejected or rights-blocked, the spine falls back to type on cloth. This covers only the six editions in the pack; it is not a random-book pipeline.

Rights and provenance

A pack is reproducible for its editions. Strict reproducibility is a different claim.

The loader verifies every file and shows the edition's rights status before it renders the art. The four modern covers are reference-only in this prototype; the two public-domain editions are cleared. Release mode demonstrates the app-side policy: restricted entries are still verified but never used for art.

Restricted reference

Harry Potter, The Lord of the Rings, The Hobbit and The Hunger Games. In copyright; this pack is for local design evaluation only. A production pack must obtain a licence or use a licensed image.

Takedown: remove the entry from the manifest and delete its three files. Clients keep the previous cached version until the next refresh, then fall back to type.

Public domain / cleared

Moby-Dick and The Hound of the Baskervilles use public-domain paintings and Standard Ebooks cover work dedicated to the public domain. These are the entries a release pack could ship today.

Update: replace the manifest and files atomically; the loader verifies the new manifest hash before swapping the IndexedDB version.

Why this is a pack feature

The app never needs to generate the art.

Data, not a pipeline

The pack is JPEGs plus JSON. Tauri Android already has a WebView, Canvas text metrics, Web Crypto and IndexedDB. Nothing here needs Python, LaMa, OCR or a desktop mask.

Integrity is explicit

The manifest's SHA-256 is checked first, then every asset. A corrupt byte is rejected before it reaches the renderer, and the edition falls back to a readable cloth spine.

Manual work stays content-side

Licensing, cover selection, text removal and spine-scale acceptance happen once, off-device. The app only downloads, verifies, caches, matches and renders. That is the pack boundary — useful, but not strict reproducibility for a random book.

Offline by construction

After one verified load the manifest and blobs live in IndexedDB. Offline reload re-hashes the cached bytes; the app shell is bundled in the APK, so only the pack needs the cache.

Conservative matching

ISBN first, then a content hash of the source cover, then title+author. A present-but-different ISBN blocks the weaker title route, so a wrong edition cannot silently claim the art.

Updates and takedown

A new latest.json carries the new manifest hash. The loader verifies before replacing the cached version; removing an entry produces the typographic fallback, never a broken spine.
