Design notes

What changed, and why

01 A wider crop, from a sharper source

At a 50 × 230 px spine, full-height art shows 31–34% of the 1400 px cover width. At 320 px it is 27–30% because the spines narrow, still roughly double v1’s 30 px, 1.45× centre crop. The old 420 px files are still here for the mode toggle; the new shelf uses 480/800 px image-set responses.

02 The printed cover title is masked, not duplicated

Standard Ebooks covers carry their own title plate in the bottom fifth. A 22%-height shelf band now covers it on every spine, replacing the cover’s type with our author and label marks. This is the main reason the new crop reads as a designed spine instead of two titles fighting.

03 Type gets a floor, not a hope

The title sits in a smoked-glass rail at ≥90% ink. Against pure white that still leaves ≥5.6:1 for cream; real covers do better. The Hound of the Baskervilles is too light for cream, so it switches to a warm vellum rail with dark ink (≥14:1). The automatic pass makes the same call for Rock Paper Scissors. Old-style keeps the failures visible as amber dots.

04 A per-cover focus point, declared in the data

The 18 classics carry a hand-declared x focus — a face, a house, a clock, a ship’s rigging — and the CSS position is solved from that focus and the spine aspect. No two books share a mechanical centre crop. The compare strip draws the resulting window on the actual cover.

05 The last two are derived, not drawn

auto-layout.cjs reads a 260 px working copy of each new cover: a column-saliency pass chooses the focus window, a text-band pass chooses the foot band and a top marketing cap, and a luminance pass chooses cream-on-ink or dark-on-vellum. It also emits the focus-score curve and the reasons behind each pass or review. No crop value for the two new arrivals is hand-set.

Still open:
  • Does the title rail hide too much of the art at 40 px on the narrowest phones?
  • Should the band carry the author, the genre chips, or both at 6 px?
  • Where does the automatic pass still need a human? Rock Paper Scissors is one clear answer: it picks the left snow/church edge, but the printed title still crosses the crop.
  • Does the three-shelf wall of six plus a two-book new-arrivals case read as a library, or does the density loss hurt the shelf metaphor?
