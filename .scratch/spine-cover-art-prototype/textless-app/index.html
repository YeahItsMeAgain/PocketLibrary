Pack feature · not strict reproducibility

Textless art,
in the app.

A downloadable Textless Art Pack replaces the desktop Python, hand masks and LaMa for the books in the pack. The manifest is keyed by content hash, ISBN and title+author; every asset is SHA-256 verified and cached offline in IndexedDB. A book outside the pack falls back to a generated plate.

No model weights on device. No OCR. No inpainting. Public-domain art only. This is a pack, not a random-book pipeline.

Integrity

Verified, not assumed

—

The manifest is checked against pack-lock.js, which ships with the app shell. Every spine and art file is hashed before it enters IndexedDB and hashed again when it is read back. A single flipped byte drops that book to the generated plate.

The split

What the pack does not solve

Pack-reproducible for these books

  • Textless spines for the books in the pack.
  • Manifest lookup by content hash, ISBN or title+author.
  • SHA-256 integrity and offline IndexedDB cache.
  • 2× spine crops generated at build time, no desktop Python at runtime.
  • Deterministic fallback plate for missing, corrupt or unknown books.

Still needs v8-app

  • Arbitrary books added by the user, with no pack entry.
  • Automatic text removal from a modern in-copyright cover.
  • On-device detection / mask / inpainting and its model download.
  • New public-domain titles: they need a pack build, not a device run.
  • A signed, hosted pack feed at scale.

Strict verdict: Textless art is not strict-reproducible for a random book. This pack reproduces spines for the 18 books in the pack; a book outside it falls back to a generated plate. Arbitrary covers remain the v8-app route.

Throwaway design prototype, not production code. Pack art is public domain; per-title credits, licence and provenance are in the manifest. Modern edition records are metadata/ISBN only and ship no pixels.
