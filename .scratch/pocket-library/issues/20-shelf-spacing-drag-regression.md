# 20: Shelf spacing and drag regression

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**What to fix:** With five books on a shelf, the wall spreads them across the full board; dragging one reflows every other book and feels like swapping rather than placing. The approved prototype keeps books packed together on the board, so the real wall should do the same.

**Category:** bug
**Status:** resolved

## Evidence

- Diagnostics report 5 books on one shelf.
- `v4-books` still uses `justify-content: space-between`.
- Drag measurement derives slot centres from a justified, full-width layout, so preview positions do not match what the user sees.

## Agent Brief

**Current behavior:** Spines are distributed evenly across the whole shelf. During a long-press drag the preview uses stale geometry from that distributed layout, so other books jump and the drop position feels like a replacement rather than an insertion.

**Desired behavior:** Books sit together on the left of the shelf in shelf order, separated by the normal spine gap. A shelf with empty capacity shows empty board on the right. Dragging a book inserts it at the pointer position like the prototype; the other books slide aside to reveal a single gap, and the drop lands exactly where the preview shows it. Cross-shelf moves keep working, as does edge auto-scroll.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A shelf with fewer books than its capacity packs them from the left, not across the full width.
- [ ] Slot geometry used by drag agrees with the packed layout.
- [ ] Dragging a book between two existing books inserts it there, before/after correctly, not merely swapping with a neighbour.
- [ ] The dragged book's source slot and the target drop gap are visible and the other books do not jump at lift or drop.
- [ ] Existing drag tests plus a new packed-layout test pass at 390×844 with no horizontal overflow.

**Out of scope:**
- Free x/y positioning of books; the catalogue still stores an ordered slot, not arbitrary coordinates.
- Changing shelf capacity or adding shelf management.

## Resolution

Books now use a packed-left shelf. Drag geometry is measured from the real spines and refreshed on each preview change, and the drop commits the visible preview index. Playwright: 110/110 including new packed-layout and middle-insertion tests.
